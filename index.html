About
Me

I'm Gurnoor Kaur — a Business, Technology & Entrepreneurship student at NYU Stern with a deep belief that the best ideas sit at the intersection of people and systems. Growing up, I saw firsthand how mental health conversations were missing in youth spaces, which led me to co-found Project Shine, a mental health awareness organization giving young people language and community around their inner lives.

My work spans leadership, communication, and the operational backbone that keeps organizations moving — from managing teams to navigating the fast-paced demands of customer-facing roles. I'm drawn to problems that matter: how we build tools, communities, and companies that actually serve people.

"Awareness is the first act of change."
